Local connection and camera setup

Join XW_Drone_WiFi using the existing password. Stay connected even if the phone reports no internet. The familiar HTTP page is at 192.168.4.1:8080; the secure camera page is at 192.168.4.1:8443.

Download the public local certificate. Verify its fingerprint against the setup record on your Mac before trusting it. Never transfer a private key. A trusted root can authenticate certificates it signs; remove it when you finish this local setup.

This is the phone's camera, not an onboard video feed. Camera and IMU data are not synchronized or spatially calibrated. Tilt uses gravity only: yaw and stabilization are unavailable. Closing or hiding this page stops camera capture.
